Prepare for Your Internship Interview

Build confidence by understanding what to expect and practicing your answers.

Before the Interview

Research the company, read recent news, study the mission statement, and understand the core internship role requirements.

STEP 1 • RESEARCH

Common Questions

Practice questions about your background, personal skills, academic projects, team experiences, and long-term career goals.

STEP 2 • PRACTICE

Answer Clearly

Use specific examples when explaining your skills and experience. Structure answers with Situation, Task, Action, and Result (STAR).

STEP 3 • STRUCTURE

Ask Questions

Prepare thoughtful questions to ask the interviewer regarding team culture, mentorship models, and upcoming project cycles.

STEP 4 • ENGAGE

Common Interview Questions

Practice answering questions you may be asked during an internship interview.

1 Tell me about yourself.
2 Why are you interested in this internship?
3 Why did you choose your field of study?
4 What skills can you bring to this internship?
5 Tell me about a project you worked on.
6 What is one challenge you have faced and how did you solve it?
7 What do you hope to learn from this internship?
8 Do you have any questions for us?

Don't Have Much Experience Yet?

That's okay. Your university projects, coursework, volunteer activities, clubs, and personal projects can help demonstrate your skills.

University Projects

Class deliverables, capstones, and lab reports demonstrate problem formulation, teamwork, execution, and presentation skills.

Include tech stack & your role

Coursework

Specialized upper-division subjects, lab modules, and academic focus areas prove baseline domain knowledge to recruiters.

List 4-6 most relevant modules

Volunteer Experience

Community outreach, campus societies, event organization, and student leadership show character, initiative, and responsibility.

Focus on leadership & coordination

Personal Projects

Independent coding applications, design portfolios, research essays, or case studies demonstrate genuine intrinsic motivation.

Provide working links / demos
